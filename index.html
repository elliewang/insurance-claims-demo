LEGAL TECHNOLOGY ENGAGEMENT

From claim intake to defensible legal resolution.

A connected legal-operations journey with policy-based escalations, evidence stewardship, workflow controls, and traceable actions.

Legal workflow orchestration

  1. Trigger & triageInjury, liability dispute, litigation or high-severity indicators route for specialist review.
  2. Case creationAssociate claim reference, matter type, owner, and deadlines in a legal-matter system.
  3. Evidence governanceCapture chain-of-custody metadata, retention rules, legal holds, and access permissions.
  4. Counsel engagementCoordinate internal/external counsel, secure communication, and budget approvals.
  5. Negotiation & settlementDocument assessments, authority checks, and approved settlement instructions.
  6. Closure & auditRecord disposition, legal spend, approvals, and retention schedule.
